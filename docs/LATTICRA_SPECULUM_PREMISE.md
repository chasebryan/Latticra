# Latticra Speculum Premise

Status: no-effect simulation-premise clarification contract
Date: 2026-05-29 CDT
Scope: companion framing for the computational-proof foundation, clarifying how Latticra may discuss simulation-bound reality research without promoting a simulation claim.

## Purpose

This record names the clarifying simulation premise as **Speculum**.

The name is intentionally paired with simulacrum. A simulacrum is the represented likeness; Speculum is the inspecting mirror. In Latticra, that means a simulation premise must expose how the representation is constructed, bounded, observed, challenged, and rejected before it can support even a narrow claim.

Speculum does not assert that reality is a simulation. It defines the interpretive boundary for research language: Latticra may model existence, observation, state transition, and physical constraint as bounded computational structure, but only as a reviewable premise until proof objects, measurements, receipts, replay, and adversarial review support a promoted conclusion.

## Clarifying Premise

The Speculum premise permits research questions such as:

```text
Can observed state be modeled as bounded computational state?
Can observer effects be represented without erasing the observer boundary?
Can physical constraints be modeled as invariants without claiming the model is the physics?
Can a state transition be replayed, falsified, and reviewed as a proof object?
```

The premise does not permit a claim jump from representation to reality:

```text
model_is_reality_claimed=0
representation_is_truth_claimed=0
simulation_premise_promoted_without_proof=0
physics_measurement_inferred_from_docs=0
metaphysical_claim_promoted=0
```
