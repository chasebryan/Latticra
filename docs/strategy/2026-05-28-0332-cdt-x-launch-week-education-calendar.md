## Purpose

This calendar turns the @Latticra audience-growth goal into a careful first week of educational content.

The goal is not high volume. The goal is visible quality: clear teaching, beautiful presentation, conservative claims, and enough spacing that each post can breathe.

## Posting Throttle

Hard rule:

```text
Do not publish outbound public content closer than about 5 hours apart unless Chase explicitly overrides the cadence for a specific event.
```

For this account, outbound public content includes:

```text
original posts
thread posts
quote posts
public replies
reposts
profile edits
pinning or unpinning
```

Default operating rhythm:

```text
1 educational post most days
2 public actions only on high-quality days, spaced at least 5 hours apart
skip a missed window instead of compressing posts
no catch-up bursts
no late-night posting unless it is intentionally scheduled
```
