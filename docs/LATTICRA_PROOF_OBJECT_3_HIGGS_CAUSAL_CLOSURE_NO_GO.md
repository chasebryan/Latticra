## Causal Closure Standard

A theory is causally closed as a first-principles mass origin only if its mass predictions are derived from its primitive state and dynamics.

For a mass set:

```text
M_obs = {m_1, m_2, ..., m_n}
```

causal closure requires:

```text

```

where:

```text
P = primitive state content
D = dynamics or update law
B = boundary and symmetry conditions
```

and no member of `M_obs` is smuggled in through an unconstrained fitted parameter.

## Higgs Mass Map

At the effective Standard Model level, the Higgs mechanism maps fields and couplings into mass terms:

```text

m_W = g v / 2
m_Z = sqrt(g^2 + g'^2) v / 2
m_H^2 = 2 lambda v^2
```

This is a powerful field-level map. It is not, by itself, a causal closure proof, because:

```text

```

## Lemma 1: Mapping Is Not Origin

If a theory maps free parameters to masses, but does not derive those parameters from prior primitives and dynamics, then it explains the representation of mass terms but not their final origin.

Proof:

Let a theory output mass values `m_i` through a map:

```text
m_i = F(theta_i)
```

where `theta_i` are free or externally fitted parameters. The question "why `m_i`?" becomes "why `theta_i`?" If the theory contains no derivation of `theta_i`, the causal burden has moved rather than closed. Therefore a parameter-to-mass map is not a final origin account.

## Lemma 2: Higgs Alone Leaves The Yukawa Ledger Open

The fermion mass pattern cannot be treated as causally closed by the Higgs mechanism alone when each fermion mass depends on an undetermined Yukawa coupling.

Proof:

For fermions:

```text
m_f = y_f v / sqrt(2)
```

The Higgs mechanism supplies the form of the relation. The values of `y_f` carry the specific mass pattern. If `y_f` are not derived, the mass pattern is not derived. Therefore a Higgs-only explanation leaves the Yukawa ledger open.

## Lemma 3: Higgs Alone Leaves The Vacuum Ledger Open

The Higgs vacuum expectation value can organize electroweak symmetry breaking without being the final causal origin of its own scale.

Proof:

The electroweak mass terms depend on `v`. If a theory uses `v` but does not derive why that scale exists with that value, then `v` is an explanatory input for the mass map. The field-level mechanism is valid, but causal closure remains incomplete.

## Lemma 4: Free-Parameter Explanations Are Non-Unique

If masses are generated by adjustable coupling constants, then multiple parameter assignments or deeper theories can reproduce the same effective mass data.

Proof:

The observed mass set constrains combinations such as `y_f v`. Without a derivation of `y_f` and `v`, the effective description does not uniquely determine the deeper cause. A non-unique effective fit cannot be the final first-principles origin. It is a successful phenomenological layer.

## Theorem: Higgs-Only Final Origin Is Refuted

A Higgs-only account is not a causally closed first-principles origin of mass.

Proof:

By Lemma 1, mapping free parameters to mass does not close causal origin. By Lemma 2, the Higgs mechanism alone leaves the Yukawa mass pattern undetermined. By Lemma 3, it leaves the vacuum scale ledger open. By Lemma 4, free-parameter effective descriptions are non-unique with respect to deeper causes. Therefore the claim that Higgs alone is the final first-principles origin of mass is refuted.
